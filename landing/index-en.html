Why a compressor that knows your platform?

Free tools compress. They don't guarantee the result passes a platform's upload rules. TinyPress turns platform rules into presets, so the pitfalls are already handled.

Presets with correct parameters

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Discord, email — each preset carries that platform's real limits. Pick one and go, no guesswork.

Images and video, one pipeline

Drop a mixed batch, process in parallel. Images use perceptual compression; video uses FFmpeg with quality-aware encoding parameters.

100% local, privacy first

Compression happens on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, nothing touches a server, and it works offline — safe for sensitive footage.

Platform limits, built in

Presets update as platform rules change — no reinstall needed.
